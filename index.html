Pipeline

How a pick is made

01

Features, strictly lagged

Every in-game statistic enters only as a rolling average of earlier games. Four independent guards assert this, and they have caught real bugs.

02

Bagged prediction with an interval

500 block-bootstrap resamples over season-week blocks give each game a probability and a 95% interval — roughly 0.16 wide.

03

De-vig the market

Shin's method removes the book's 3.7% margin. Comparing against a raw implied probability would invent edge on every bet.

04

Require the interval to clear it

One-sided: the whole interval must sit above the fair price. A point estimate beating the market is never enough.

Walk-forward cross-validation, time-based split, 2025 held out and touched once. No random k-fold anywhere.
